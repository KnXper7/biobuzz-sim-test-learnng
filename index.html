Rules the sim enforces

  • G408 Don't hold blue NECTAR. Spit it out.
  • G410 NECTAR into a FLOWER only in the last 60 s (MAJOR FOUL, 20).
  • G418 Only take POLLEN out of the bottom of a FLOWER.
  • G426/427 Human player enters 1 NECTAR per HIVE TIP, or all of them with 60 s left (MINOR FOUL, 5). Press N.

What Java the simulator understands
  • Classes with fields, constructors, methods, static final constants, simple enums, LinearOpMode and iterative OpMode.
  • Hardware: DcMotor/DcMotorEx, Servo, CRServo, IMU, DistanceSensor, TouchSensor, ElapsedTime, Range, telemetry, gamepad1/gamepad2.
  • Not modeled: interfaces, inner/anonymous classes, generics beyond simple lists, integer division (5/2 gives 2.5 here, 2 in Java), vision, Road Runner. Android Studio is still the real compiler: always build there before a match.

Robot configuration (what the Control Hub knows)

One device per line: type name [slot]. Names must match the strings in hardwareMap.get() exactly. Slots FL/FR/BL/BR place drive motors on the chassis. Types: motor, servo, crservo, imu, distance, touch.
